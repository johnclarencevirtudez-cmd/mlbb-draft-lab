Ban decision checklist

  • Is the hero currently strong in this patch and rank bracket?
  • Can the opponent first-pick it?
  • Does it invalidate your planned composition?
  • Do you have a reliable counter, and can you execute it?
  • Is the ban supported by current pick/ban data?

🗺️ Map & rotation framework

Before Turtle / Lord

  1. Identify which lanes can push first.
  2. Track enemy jungler and missing heroes.
  3. Clear nearby waves before grouping.
  4. Secure river entrances and deny bushes.
  5. Trade the opposite side if contesting is losing.

Rotation principles

  • Roamer: create information and numbers advantages.
  • Mid: clear efficiently, then move with jungler/roamer.
  • Jungle: path toward the lane with setup and objective value.
  • Side lanes: manage waves before rotating.
